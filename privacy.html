Privacy

Hill Mole runs no analytics, sets no tracking cookies, shows no ads, and has no forms or comments. Nothing on these pages watches you read.

your device

The dark button in the banner saves your choice in your browser under hillmole-theme. It never leaves your device.

hosting

GitHub Pages serves the site and may log visitors' IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement. Everything on the page comes from this site.

amazon links

The links to the Kindle edition on the supporting pages carry Chris Abraham's Amazon Associates tag, which Amazon uses to credit purchases. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
