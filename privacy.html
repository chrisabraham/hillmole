Privacy

Hill Mole shows no ads and has no forms or comments. Since October 16, 2026, it counts readers with Google Analytics, set up as narrowly as Google allows.

analytics

Google Analytics 4 shows how many people read the novel, which entries they read, and how they found it. Advertising features, ad storage, and Google signals are off for everyone, so nothing here targets ads. Readers in the European Union, the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland get no analytics cookies, and Google receives only cookieless pings from them; the same goes for anyone whose browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Everyone else may get Google's first-party _ga cookies, which hold a random identifier so a returning reader is counted once. The script waits until you scroll, tap, or type, or four seconds after the page loads. Block it however you like, including with Google's opt-out add-on, and every page reads the same. Google's policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

your device

The dark button in the banner saves your choice in your browser under hillmole-theme. It never leaves your device.

hosting

GitHub Pages serves the site and may log visitors' IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement. Apart from Google Analytics, everything on the page comes from this site.

amazon links

The links to the Kindle edition on the supporting pages carry Chris Abraham's Amazon Associates tag, which Amazon uses to credit purchases. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
