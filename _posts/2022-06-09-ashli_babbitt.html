My question is is why does the January 6 narrative insist backwards and forwards that 5 people were killed on the Capitol? One casual scratch at that and it’s only Ashli Babbitt. What’s the compulsion? To prove that people will believe anything they’re told? And people do believe it, too. Even with a Washington Post article saying otherwise. I’m amazed by the human mind and the human mob mentality, even though it’s always on display.
