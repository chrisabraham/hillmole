Nobody gets shot for the reason they were pulled over, only for what happens after the police lights come on. It’s the escalation, it’s the evasion, it’s the erratic behavior, and it’s the not following directions and orders. It’s also about tone, what you say, and defiance. Maybe all those things should be added to the talk as well. Because complying while calling the cop a pig and making squealing sounds isn’t in the spirit of being deferential, respectful, or compliant.
