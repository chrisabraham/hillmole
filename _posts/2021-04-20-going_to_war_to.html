You know what happens when you invade a country and destabilize it over the course of twenty years? Widows, orphans, women, mothers, and children suffer, more so when they’re branded American sympathizers. This is what’s called the most abusive of marriages, America in Afghanistan. There will be blood… on our hands, like usual.
