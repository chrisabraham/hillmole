How does one spend enough money to undo systemic anything if there just isn’t any will outside of the choir and the choir is already passionately anti-racist? Also, what’s the chance that using too much stick and not enough carrot might result in a racism that’s so deeply set that it’s untreatable, resistant to any and all treatments. Which actually gets worse and worse the more it’s treated—out of spite, maybe. And, worse, what happens when these folks, who react this way, number 80+ million of your own fellow citizens. How does one grapple with that? Can one spend any amount of money to cure that? What’re the therapies one can develop to solve that one?
