An increase in gun crime always drives gun sales and applications for carry permits in America rather than driving gun restrictions or gun bans. Unintended consequences gone wild. First aware of this in the wake of the shooting of Gabby Giffords in 2011. I was driving cross country and checked all the gun stores and everyone was out of Glock 19s, the weapon the shooter used. Plus, law suits end up in courts that result in gun prohibitions being loosened. Every time.
