80% of Americans would rather be Brazil than Australia; also, none of those countries have any real “natural rights granted by God not to be infringed,” only rights granted to them by their governments only when convenient. Fair weather friends. 400 million guns, most of those ARs. This is a dangerous game since only 20% of all Americans say they trust the government as of 2020. Which means 8 out of 10 do not. Hillbilly Americans are not an outlier no matter what the New Yorker experiences from within their bubble.
