The price increase of animal protein couldn’t be social engineering, it surely must be purely invisible-hand economic. This isn’t a so-called “nudge” towards plant-based nutrition through putting animal products financially out of reach of most people. That’s ludicrous… and brilliant! Same with fossil fuels: it’s the market, stupid. The inflation’s temporary. Issues of trade and logistics only. Chill?
