Even dumb hicks know that CRT is a lens through which history is interpreted and taught in school and not actually something that is actually taught in school. Any clever parry that “Virginia does not teach CRT in K-12 public schools.” It comes across as sneaky and sus. Even dumb hicks can see through that rhetorical sleight of hand. It’s too clever by half.
