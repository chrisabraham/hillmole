I guess it's too early to be grateful for those 20-years. I mean, most of the region never even had any of that. It's hard to be grateful at all when everything you've built is stolen from you, though. That's too true after floods, fires, hurricanes, tornadoes, and earthquakes, as well. Any devastation, really. Also, after a family member is taken from you. Any life-rocking loss, grief, trauma.
