So, let me get this straight: print, radio, and television has a plan to protect its narrative hegemony by censoring social media and internet platforms? Makes sense to me. Monopoly say what? Totally a monopolist thing to do: discredit, undermine, demonize, and destroy the competition using the laws and tools of the government to repress and suppress unapproved or countermanding narratives. Sounds like it’ll totally work.
