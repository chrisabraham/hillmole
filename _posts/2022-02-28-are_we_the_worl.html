The level of shameless we are the world propaganda is so rich, thick, endless, and uncritical of itself that it’s stressing even me out and I’m probably a nihilist and nonplussed by most global behavior. My only constant is being profoundly anti-war but also being profoundly anti “liberal democracy by force” as well. It’s a direct analog to a sort of 11th century Crusades, just replacing Rome with America (fuck yeah?). It’s crazy-making to me. I’m not listening to the news anymore. Yikes!
