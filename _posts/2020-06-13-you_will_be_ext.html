When my firearms instructor told me how to engage while concealed carrying a personal firearm (at that point, I carried a Gen 3 Glock 23 in .40 S&W, a very formidable firearm often used by law enforcement), she told me that, legally, first of all, that it’s completely illegal to ever let anyone around me know that I am carrying a firearm. That the CCW permit is, itself, an explicit agreement between the County, the State, and every State with reciprocity, that you will be extremely discreet with your gun carry. One of the biggest issues is with “printing.” Printing is the visible panty line or visible nipples of gun carry. People solve it a couple of ways: getting a smaller gun, getting a holster that can conceal your pistol as deeply and completely, or off-body in a bag or purse, or it is often solved through clothing choices. People in the space call them garments, which I personally find amusing. Who calls their clothing “garments?” Things like loose overshirts or blazers or jackets or loose button-downs.
