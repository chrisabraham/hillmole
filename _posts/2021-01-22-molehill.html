Making a Mountain out of a Molehill Meaning Definition: Making a small, unimportant issue into a big problem. If someone makes a mountain out of a molehill, he is taking a little problem and turning into something much bigger and more problematic than it actually is. This phrase is used to tell someone that he or she is overreacting.
