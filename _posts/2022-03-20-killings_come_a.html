The technologies to kill efficiently has come a long way since 1945. Nukes exist. Biologicals can be targeted to DNA. Satellites exist. Subs are better. There are jets now. Helicopters now. Drones now. Smart bombs. Not even the AR or AK existed in 1945. So much potential death!
