People who read Maus realize that not every holocaust will look the same and that every holocaust starts uniquely, subtly, organically, and pretty benignly, generally initiated by people you admire and trust. It’s not a switch. It’s a cool pot of water sitting on a low flame. It happens right under your nose, especially if and when you’re being willfully ignorant and actively blind.
