I lived in Berlin for a year before I was at Hauptbahnhof at right the perfect time to realise that not all Germans were urbane, sophisticated, extremely cultured, and even painfully lovely. There were suburban German hooligans everywhere with their milk crates filled with pilsner on the S and U being all loutish and singing soccer shanties.
