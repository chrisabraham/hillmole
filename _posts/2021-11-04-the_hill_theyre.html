If America was ever truly vulnerable to actual “civil war,” I thought that the straw that broke the camel’s back would be gun confiscation but it actually might be vaccine mandates. I have never seen more people on that side of the aisle who are willing to die on that hill. Vaccine mandates might just be the hill they’re truly willing to die on.
