“Stop struggling,” he said to me when I started hyperventilating, “nobody will know unless you tell. And you can’t tell anyone, you know. What would you tell someone anyway? You really don’t know anything yourself. You haven’t done anything. Yet. And we’re only talking. There’s nothing there there. It’s your mind playing tricks on you. Its spinning into infinite loop. If you don’t pull your shit together, you’re going to have a core dump which is never pretty.”
