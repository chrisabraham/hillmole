When you live in an immigrant nation like the United States, every family has its own stories of extreme trauma and suffering. That’s mostly why people used to come to America. Not just to become richer but to escape every version of trauma imaginable. It’s extremely hard to win the Trauma Wars when America is a traumatized country with epigenetic trauma out the wazoo.
