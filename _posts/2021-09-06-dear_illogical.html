Dear illogical talking heads on the brink of two decades post-9/11: don’t you think that radical islamic militants might be less likely to commit terrorist acts on American soil now that we’re defeated and have left? When I’m occupied and being peppered with missiles and bombs by traumatic drones and aviation, I’m more likely to resort to asymmetric warfare that includes destination terrorism.
