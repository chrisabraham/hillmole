I finally see that there are two dominant modalities in the USA and never the twain shall meet: folks who believe that COVID is the worst thing in the world and folks who think it isn’t remotely the worst thing in the world. They just don’t understand each other: COVID kills children, says one site; pish posh says the other. No comprehension. They’re both completely mutually appalled.
