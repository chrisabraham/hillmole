Narrator: Chapter 48. Fire Can Lick Behind These Eyes.
Narrator: I guess after all of that training even though it’s been about twenty years ago, I do believe I was trained well to smell a tail, to smell someone who’s got evil on his mind. To feel out someone’s intent, and to hide all of that savviness behind the sweetest, most mild exterior you could ever imagine. But fire can lick behind these eyes; a growl can pass these lips.
