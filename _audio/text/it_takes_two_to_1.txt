Narrator: Chapter 140. It Takes Two to Keep a Secret.
Narrator: When you are cheating on a spouse or significant other, you are lying to someone, being private from someone—someone who knows you even better in some cases than you know yourself. When one maintains discretion, when one maintains privacy, it is essential to spend as much energy as possible avoiding attention or from bringing attention to yourself. Once you’re under scrutiny, human nature makes it nearly impossible to remain discreet, to keep the secret, to keep on message, to maintain believability, and to keep from contradicting yourself or paint yourself into a corner.
