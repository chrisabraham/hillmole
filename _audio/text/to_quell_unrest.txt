Narrator: Chapter 63. To Quell Unrest and to Avoid Widespread Panic.
Narrator: “You don’t want to know” and “most intelligence and operations work is spent to quell unrest and to avoid widespread panic” are two of the first things I am going to forget. I have been working like a madman trying to, to no avail. The next thing I want to forget is that we are the children of America and our government is our parents. And it is their job to aggressively and brutally save and protect us. And they have been doing a fantastic job until 9/11. What I want to forget is that I know that these days I have come to know that it is not just the children who are scared of the dark or what’s under the bed, but the parents are as well. I would like to forget that the most.
