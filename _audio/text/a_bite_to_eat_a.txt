Narrator: Chapter 40. A Bite to Eat at Zack’s.
Narrator: I went to Zack’s for a bite to eat because I was hungry and especially thirsty. I don’t like to think of myself as a drunk, but lately, in spite of my daily runs, I have been drinking quite a lot these days. Most of it has been a way to get out of the apartment and a lot of it has been to get out of myself. The strange things happening as of late have really put the kind of pressure on me that has made me want to get outside myself and get outside of the house. Things have been getting way too weird. What does all of this mean?
