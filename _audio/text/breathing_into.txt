Narrator: Chapter 119. Breathing Into A Brown Paper Sack.
Narrator: The fucker actually gave me a brown paper sack to breathe into. I breathed into a sack that vaguely tasted of onion and calmed right down. “It’s a panic attack,” he said finally, “and a really bad sign.” The lights were still low and everything was indistinct except for his eyes which were both calm and cruel. I felt like an ass breathing into a bag but I didn’t like the sound of really bad sign so I wanted to bide my time a little. I knew him pretty well and he is always calm but this was the first time I knew the cruel outside of braggadocio. But here it was and all I had was this motherfucking limp brown paper sack to protect me.
