Narrator: If that panicked diver threatens to bolt to the surface, risking decompression sickness (the bends) or a lung overexpansion injury, you’re supposed to stay beyond arm’s length and look at the eyes and look for panic. Try to calm from over an arm’s length.
Narrator: Then, if they look like they’re going to bolt, come around the back and grab their tank tightly to prevent them from ascending too quickly, risking injury. But, be sure to keep your hoses and mask and self away from flailing arms and hands.
