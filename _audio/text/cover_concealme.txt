Narrator: Chapter 105. Cover, Concealment, and Camouflage.
Narrator: When it comes to methods of hiding secrets, camouflage is under-appreciated. Camouflage is the art of hiding in plain sight. In my experience, what you don’t see is always more secure than something you can’t see or can’t touch but know it’s there. If you know there is something there somewhere, you can easily develop a search and recovery strategy. If you know where something is but its access is restricted, it is simple to develop a break-in strategy. The human mind is brilliant in recognizing patterns, but where pattern is not perceived, there is nothing. The human mind cannot see what it cannot see; or rather, the mind cannot perceive what it cannot recognize.
