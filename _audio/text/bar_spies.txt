Narrator: Chapter 101. Bar Spies.
Narrator: If you want to spot a spy, you have to see him work the bar when he’s drinking. Even though your garden-variety spy is only 5-foot-9 and 150-pounds, he gets laid a lot. Women are attracted to fearlessness. Spies are unable to hold their liquor enough to mask this sex-appeal. If he is seriously popular with the ladies and hasn’t obvious power, wealth, or beauty, bet he’s a spy.
