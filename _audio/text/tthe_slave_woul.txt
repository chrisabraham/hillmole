Narrator: Chapter 289. The slave would rise in rebellion.
Narrator: “But if there were a man who had sufficient force, he would shake off and break through, and escape from all this; he would trample under foot all our formulas and spells and charms, and all our laws which are against nature: the slave would rise in rebellion and be lord over us, and the light of natural justice would shine forth.”
Narrator: —Callicles
