History

1988
Chris moves to Washington, DC for George Washington University.
1989–1990
Works as a bike courier in DC. These years become the novel's raw material.
1990–1991
Study abroad at the University of East Anglia's School of English and American Studies, Norwich. Spends every long weekend and break in Paris, taking the train and ferry, staying in hostels, and hosting a salon at Shakespeare and Company under George Whitman.
Early 1991
Two weeks in Kathmandu with his best mate, Mark, on cheap flights during Operation Desert Storm, exploring the jungles and foothills of the Kathmandu Valley.
1991–1992
A year off in Hawaii learning French at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, the year after spending every break in Paris without speaking a word of it.
1993
Graduates from GW with a BA in American Literature, the last one the university awarded.
May 13, 2005
First entry, written in a basement apartment at 14th and A Streets SE.
2007–2010
Lives in Berlin, still writing.
2011
Drives around the country, then settles in Portland, Oregon.
2012
Settles in South Arlington, off Columbia Pike.
June 11, 2022
Last entry.
August 15, 2024
Kindle edition published.
October 4, 2026
Ported from Movable Type CGI to GitHub Pages.
