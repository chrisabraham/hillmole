Upon Meeting My Own Mata Hari

I admit that my work sounds intriguing, especially when you live in a city rife with spies and spycraft. When I tell people that I am in marketing but that I have my own company and it is virtual and that my employees live spread around the world and that I have found some success, it confounds people. When I take it further and discuss some of the more fun parts of what I do -- reputation management, online persuasion, information crowd control, hearts and minds promotional tactics, and memetic engineering, I always attract the attention of people who, were I a paranoid or delusional man, I might fancy being spies who have been given me and my associates as reconnaissance details.

Half a year ago I took a client who is an oligarch and a businessman -- or, rather, he was. This work was intriguing even to me as my job was to remove his brushes with International courts and his scrapes with the leaders of his former Soviet Union. This was no easy game because while I am very good at what I do with online brand reputation management and repair (I am a cleaner), I knew that my work was being countermanded both by a communications team in Russia and by a wide assortment of articles and post that made it a constant challenge to both gain ground and hold it in the search engines.

What is when Ada, Romanian beauty, appeared at a local happy hour I attend. To me, she is perfect; however, I have a very odd taste in women. She is a very petite brunette who holds a doctorate in infectious disease.

When we met, we spoke about memetics, a theory that cutlure is like a virus that is contagious and can mutate to adjust to its environment. This dovetailed into talk about propaganda and and then into infectious diseases and her work. She stayed late, well after her friend left and I was able to collect her phone number and then walk her to the train.

I had a fleeting thought in my head that she was too good to be true.
