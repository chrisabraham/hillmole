A Hill Mole short story from Google Bard AI

I was merely a dupe and a fool. I had been working in the private sector in Washington, DC, for eleven years, and I had never been an employee of the government nor had I worked in the military. The closest thing I had ever had to a security clearance was the light review the Secret Service made in order to let me work as a contractor to the Treasury Department.

I had always thought of myself as a smart and independent person, but I had been completely unaware of the greater plan that I was a part of. I had been used, unwittingly, to further the interests of those in power.

I began to realize what was happening when I was approached by a man who claimed to be from the CIA. He told me that I had been selected for a special project, and that I would be playing a vital role in national security. I was flattered and excited, and I eagerly agreed to help.

The man gave me a series of tasks to complete, and I did them without question. I was told that I was helping to gather intelligence on a potential terrorist threat, and I was proud to be doing my part to protect my country.

But as time went on, I began to have doubts. I started to wonder why I was being kept in the dark about what I was really working on. I also started to notice that the man who had recruited me was always vague about his answers to my questions.

One day, I finally decided to confront the man. I demanded to know what was really going on, and I told him that I was no longer willing to participate in his games.

The man was furious. He told me that I was a fool, and that I had no idea what I was talking about. He said that I was in over my head, and that I would be better off just keeping my mouth shut.

I was scared, but I stood my ground. I told the man that I was not going to be a part of his lies anymore. I walked away, and I never looked back.

It took me a long time to come to terms with what had happened. I felt betrayed and used, and I was angry at myself for being so naive. But eventually, I came to realize that I had done the right thing. I had stood up for what I believed in, even when it was difficult.

I am still not sure what the man was really up to, but I know that I was part of something bigger than myself. I was a pawn in a game that I did not understand, but I am proud of myself for refusing to play along.

I am no longer a dupe or a fool. I am an independent thinker, and I will not be used by anyone.
