Excerpt of The Mole, Written by Google Bard AI

I was a mole. I had been planted in the government by a secret organization that was working to expose corruption and wrongdoing. I had been working undercover for years, and I had learned a lot about the inner workings of the government. I had seen things that would make your blood run cold.

I had seen politicians accepting bribes from lobbyists. I had seen government officials awarding contracts to their friends and family members. I had seen waste and fraud on a massive scale.

I knew that I had to do something to stop it. I couldn't just sit back and watch the government continue to be corrupted. I had to speak out.

I knew that it would be dangerous. I knew that I could be killed or imprisoned for what I was doing. But I didn't care. I had to do what was right.

I started by leaking information to the press. I told them about the corruption I had seen. I gave them evidence. I helped them to expose the truth.

The public was outraged. They demanded that the government be held accountable. The government was forced to investigate the allegations.

In the end, several politicians were forced to resign. Several government officials were indicted. Millions of dollars in taxpayer money was recovered.

I had done it. I had exposed the corruption and brought the government to justice. I had made a difference.

I was a mole, but I was also a hero.
